01

Overview

Lethal Ape Extended ("the game," "we," "us") is a multiplayer VR game built for Meta Quest headsets. This policy explains what data gets collected when you play, why we collect it, and which outside services help us run the game — namely Meta (platform and store), PlayFab (accounts, matchmaking backend, and live ops), and Photon (real-time networking for multiplayer sessions).

By installing or playing Lethal Ape Extended, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the game.

02

Information we collect

Account & platform data

  • Your Meta account ID and display name, provided to us by the Meta Platform SDK when you launch the game.
  • Headset and hardware type (e.g. Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest Pro), used to tune performance and locomotion comfort settings.
  • App version, session length, and crash logs.

Gameplay & progression data

  • In-game profile: unlocked cosmetics, stats, achievements, and progression, stored via PlayFab.
  • Matchmaking data: region, lobby history, and session results.
  • Movement telemetry from the game's arm-swing locomotion system (position/velocity of your tracked hands and headset), used only to drive your in-game movement and never sent anywhere as raw recorded footage.

Networking data

  • Your device's IP address and connection quality metrics, handled by Photon to host and sync multiplayer rooms.
  • Real-time position, rotation, and action data for your avatar, relayed to other players in your session so the game can sync movement.

Communications

  • If voice chat is enabled, live audio is transmitted peer-to-peer or relayed through Photon Voice for the duration of a session. We do not record or store voice chat.
03

Third-party services we rely on

We don't build our own servers for identity, matchmaking, or networking — we rely on established platforms. Each one processes a slice of your data under its own privacy terms:

Meta Platforms, Inc.
Distribution · Account identity · Store

Meta provides the Quest platform SDK we use for login, entitlement checks (verifying you own the game), and store integration. Meta's handling of your account data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

PlayFab (Microsoft)
Player accounts · Backend · Live ops · Analytics

PlayFab stores your player profile, inventory, statistics, and title data, and powers matchmaking and any live events or store offers inside the game. PlayFab is a Microsoft product; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how it handles data on our behalf.

Photon Engine (Exit Games GmbH)
Real-time networking · Voice relay

Photon hosts the relay servers that sync players inside a multiplayer room — position, movement, actions, and optionally voice. Photon processes connection metadata (like IP address) needed to route traffic. See Photon's privacy policy for details.

We do not currently send player data to any advertising or analytics network beyond what's built into the above platforms. If that changes, we'll update this policy and the version number at the top of this page.

04

How we use your data

  • To run core gameplay: locomotion, matchmaking, room hosting, and syncing your avatar with other players.
  • To save your progression, cosmetics, and settings between sessions.
  • To detect and act on cheating, exploits, or abusive behavior reported through in-game tools.
  • To diagnose crashes and performance issues.
  • To communicate important updates, such as patch notes or service interruptions, where you've opted in.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your gameplay data to build advertising profiles.

05

Voice & multiplayer data

Lethal Ape Extended is played with other people in real time. When you're in a multiplayer lobby, your avatar's movement and, if enabled, your voice are shared live with everyone else in that room through Photon's relay servers. This is necessary for the game to function — there's no way to play multiplayer without other players seeing your movement and hearing your voice chat.

You can mute yourself or other players, and disable voice chat entirely, from the in-game settings menu at any time.

07

Data retention

Your PlayFab player profile and progression are kept for as long as your account is active. Session-based networking data held by Photon (positions, voice audio) exists only for the duration of a live match and is not stored afterward. If you delete your account, we'll remove your PlayFab profile within [30] days, aside from records we're legally required to keep.

08

Security

We rely on the security infrastructure of Meta, PlayFab, and Photon, all of which encrypt data in transit. Access to player data on our end is limited to team members who need it to operate the game. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute protection against every possible breach.

09

Children's privacy

Lethal Ape Extended is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your Meta account), and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them beyond what Meta's platform itself requires for account creation. If you believe a child has provided us data outside of Meta's own account system, contact us using the details in Section 13 and we'll remove it.

10

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights:

  • Manage your Meta account data through your Meta Quest privacy settings.
  • Request deletion of your in-game PlayFab profile by contacting us at badbatterystudios@gmail.com.
  • Mute or disable voice chat at any time from the pause menu.

Residents of the EEA/UK and California have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively — see Section 13 to reach us with a request.

11

International transfers

Because Meta, PlayFab, and Photon operate global server infrastructure, your data may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Each of those providers maintains its own safeguards for cross-border transfers, described in the links in Section 3.
